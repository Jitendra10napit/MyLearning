### Architect
 

Definition
 

He is responsible for making important decisions about how a project is designed and built.
He closely work with the delivery team to create the overall plan for how the project will work.
He is concerned with the high level technical design and functionality of the product.
He provides the technical leadership to the development team & time to time review the work to avoid risk
 

High level design like

 

Component design
Data design
Interface design
Security design
Performance design
Etc.
 

 

 

## Responsibilities
 


Understands the requirements of the architecture and system as well as the functionality and performance of the other products in the system.
Defines the top level system design of the product.
Liaises closely with Design Authorities and Lead Designers assigned to projects in the same architecture.
Provides constant and careful attention to technical leadership and oversight of projects
Assists development teams in assessing and avoiding risk
Defines which the development tools, practices and technologies used in the projects.
Develops relationships with the NICE product management team and with major technical partners.
Reviews the design of the Product with the Design Authority, Systems Engineer and Developers.

## The Enterprise Architect (EA):

 

Owns the product and software architecture within their domain
Works with the PO and PM to ensure architectural items are included in the domain roadmap
Defines the tools and technologies within their domain
Ensures consistency of approach with other domain teams, attending the solution architectural forum
Works within the architectural strategy as defined by the Solution Architecture Team
Is responsible for ensuring good code quality within their domain
Works together with the Test Lead to define the test strategy for their domain
Promotes innovation within their domain team
Is often the Security Architect for their domain




## Day-to-Day Responsibilities

1. Collaboration with PO & PM

Prioritize architectural items (infra, security, tech upgrades)
Align roadmap with technical feasibility
Identify risks, dependencies early
2. Architecture Ownership

Own the software architecture of the application/domain
Ensure scalability, reliability, and maintainability
Create/maintain architectural artefacts (HL design, diagrams)
3. High-Level Feature Design

Break down features into high-level design
Identify dependencies, integrations, tools, and components
Provide guidance to dev team for implementation
4. Tools, Technologies, and Guidelines

Define tech stack, frameworks, libraries
Set coding standards, architecture guidelines
Ensure consistency across teams and with Solution Architect’s direction
5. Code Quality & Reviews

Oversee code review process
Enforce clean code, patterns, and best practices
Ensure quality and maintainability of codebase
6. Test Strategy Collaboration

Work with Test Lead to define L1, L2, L3 testing strategy
Ensure test coverage aligns with architecture
Promote shift-left testing mindset
7. Security Responsibilities

Act as security architect for the team
Implement and enforce secure coding practices
Static code scans (SAST), periodic Black Duck scans (SCA)
Enable GitHub Advanced Security for early vulnerability detection
8. Cross-Domain Alignment

Ensure architectural consistency across domains
Work with Solution Architect for governance & standards
